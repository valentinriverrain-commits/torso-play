# Torso — Play

Interactive 3D versions of the **Torso Electronics T-1** (algorithmic sequencer) and **S-4** (sculpting sampler), playable in the browser.

**Live demo:** https://valentinriverrain-commits.github.io/torso-play/

- **T-1** — 4 tracks of Euclidean rhythms. The 18 knobs (with their CTRL second functions), pads, PLAY, CLEAR, CTRL, BANK, PATTERN, TEMP and MUTE behave like the real instrument.
- **S-4** — 4 stereo tracks, each through the five devices DISC · MOSAIC · RING · DEFORM · VAST, plus MIX, MOD and PERFORM views, scenes and the △□⬡○ action buttons. The screen is drawn live.

Sound is synthesised in the browser (Web Audio). Models made in Blender and exported to glTF; rendering with three.js.

**Keys:** Space play · Tab switch instrument · 1–4 tracks · Shift = second function (T-1) · Q W E R T / M O P views (S-4).

Runs as a static page: open `index.html` (double-click works) or serve the folder.
